What's new

2.0.0

  • Flashcards are here, working the way they do in Anki and AnkiDroid. Make decks and subdecks, then study them with Again, Hard, Good, and Easy. Each button shows when you'll see the card again.
  • Add a kanji or a word to a deck from its page, or fill a deck from a category, a folder, or a JLPT level in one go.
  • While studying, you can undo your last answer, and bury or suspend a card. When you finish a deck for the day, it tells you when the next card is ready.
  • Each deck has Anki's deck options: how many new cards and reviews a day, learning steps, FSRS, and the rest. The ⓘ next to each section explains its options in Anki's own words.
  • Bring your decks with you: import Anki deck files (.apkg) on your phone or computer, or, on your phone, import your decks and progress straight from AnkiDroid 2.24 or later.
  • Backups now include your flashcards. Syncing your phone and computer brings your flashcards and their pictures along too.
  • Settings has a new Flashcards section, with the hour a new day starts and how far ahead learning cards may show.
  • The app now checks once a day, when it starts, whether a newer version is out, and tells you. You can turn this off under About in Settings.

1.2.0

  • A kanji's page shows its JLPT level and how many strokes it has, under the kanji.
  • The bar for moving between screens now has Kanji, Learn, and Flashcards. Learn and Flashcards are coming soon.
  • Categories, Folders, the kana charts, and Settings have moved to the ⋯ menu at the top of the Kanji screen.
  • You can hide rōmaji, the Japanese written in Latin letters: use the あa button at the top of a kanji or word page, and the あ button to show it again. The kana charts always show it.
  • The app's color is now green (Matcha). If you chose another color under Accent in Settings, you keep it.
  • The credits, under About in Settings, now name the developer and thank the tester.
