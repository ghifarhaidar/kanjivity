What's new

1.2.0

  • A kanji's page shows its JLPT level and how many strokes it has, under the kanji.
  • The bar at the bottom now has Kanji, Learn, and Flashcards. Learn and Flashcards are coming soon.
  • Categories, Folders, the kana charts, and Settings have moved to the ⋯ menu at the top of the Kanji screen.
  • You can hide rōmaji: tap あa at the top of a kanji or word page, and tap あ to show it again. The kana charts always show it.
  • The app is green (Matcha) unless you picked another accent in Settings.
  • The credits name the developer and thank the tester.
