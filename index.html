Kanjivity

A Japanese–English dictionary for Android, Windows, and Linux that works offline.

  • Kanji with stroke order, readings in kana and rōmaji, meanings, and the words written with them.
  • Search by kanji, kana, rōmaji, or English, conjugated forms included.
  • Your own categories and folders, with backups and sync between phone and computer over your local network.
