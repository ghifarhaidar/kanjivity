Privacy policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

Summary

Kanjivity collects no personal data. It has no accounts, ads, analytics, or crash reporting, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your categories and folders, and your settings: theme, accent, and backup choices.

Backups

Backups are files written only to your backup folder: on a computer, a backups folder beside the app's data unless you choose another; on Android, a folder you choose.

Google backup on Android

If Google backup is on, Android backs up your categories and folders to your Google account, and nothing else from the app. That backup is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Sync

Sync exchanges categories and folders directly between your phone and your computer over your local network, encrypted. It runs only while the computer's Sync screen is open, and it goes through no server.

Permissions

  • Camera: asked the first time you scan a sync code. Its frames are read on the device to find the code, and are never saved or sent.
  • Network access: used only for sync on the local network.

Where you download

The store or GitHub you download Kanjivity from has its own privacy policy.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses, as GitHub's privacy statement describes. The website has no cookies, scripts, or analytics.

Children

Since nothing is collected, nothing is collected from children.

Changes

A change is published on this page with a new date.
